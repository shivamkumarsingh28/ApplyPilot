# Code of conduct

We want ApplyPilot to be a friendly place to learn and build, especially for people early in their careers.

- Be respectful and patient. Questions are welcome; there are no silly questions.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person.
- No harassment, insults, or discrimination of any kind.
- Respect privacy. Never share other people's personal data or documents, even as test data.

Maintainers may remove comments or contributions that break these rules. Report problems to the maintainers by email (add your address here before publishing).
